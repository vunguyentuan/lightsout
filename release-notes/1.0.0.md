- Keeps your Mac awake and its session unlocked, so UI-automation agents keep working through the night.
- Lights Out turns every screen black and the backlight off (built-in, Apple and DDC monitors), while screenshots and screen recordings still see the real desktop.
- Moving the real mouse or pressing a key brings the screen back; input from agents doesn't.
- Goes dark on its own after a few minutes without you, toggles with ⌃⌥⌘L, and takes `lightsout://` commands from scripts.
