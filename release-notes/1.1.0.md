- **Stay Awake with Lid Closed:** on power, your MacBook keeps running with the lid shut even after you switch the external monitor off. Turn it on from the menu and allow Lights Out once in System Settings. On battery, or when Lights Out quits, a closed lid sleeps the Mac as usual.
- **Brightness sliders** in the menu set each display's brightness, including external monitors that support DDC/CI.
- **Auto Brightness** can follow an external light sensor, so brightness tracks the room even in clamshell, where the Mac's own sensor is covered.
- Scripts can use `lightsout://lid-awake/on`, `lightsout://brightness/50` and `lightsout://auto-brightness/on`.
